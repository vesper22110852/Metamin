About the researcher

Curious about light. Serious about evidence.

I’m Seokmin Kim.

I am a nanophotonics researcher interested in metasurfaces, inverse design, and computational electromagnetics. My work focuses on translating electromagnetic mechanisms into optical responses that remain useful under real design constraints.

I work across RCWA and FDTD: using efficient spectral models to explore design spaces, full-wave fields to test explanations, and optimization to discover structures that intuition alone may not reach.

Research interests

Metasurfaces · Inverse design · Structural color · Cavity optics · Computational electromagnetics

Methods

RCWA · FDTD · Spectral analysis · Field analysis · Numerical optimization
