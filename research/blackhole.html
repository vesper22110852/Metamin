Project Blackhole

In progress

Broadband white OLED, RGB metamirrors and a black matrix.

Working principle

01

White OLED stack

A shared OLED stack supplies broadband white emission. The joined layers and narrow white emissive band are schematic; no specific emitter sequence is shown.

02

RGB Fabry–Pérot cavity

The lower metamirrors and upper output interface form an optical cavity. Its Fabry–Pérot response is designed to favor the red, green and blue bands in top emission.

03

Black matrix

Ring–disk absorbers occupy the boundaries between square metamirror regions. They absorb the portion of incident broadband light reaching these boundaries, reducing unwanted boundary reflection.

Design goal RGB-selective cavity emission from the metamirror regions, with broadband absorption at their boundaries.

Only the concept is shown; material choices, dimensions and performance data are omitted.
